# V1 workout and decomposition library

## Content rule

Official station requirements and product-designed practice drills are different data.
Every drill below is an **approximation** unless performed with verified equipment and
the official sequence. The app must show what the drill develops and which station it
maps to; it must not label substitute success as official-test success.

Repetitions, rounds, and load are plan variables selected from recent completion and the
current phase. Do not hard-code maximal volume or prescribe failure sets.

## Session A - police technique and cognition

Default load: low to moderate. Default duration: 40-60 minutes.

Components:

1. One active memory game (video detective, shuffled ordering, rule matching, transition
   choice, or error spotting).
2. Station-2 obstacle confidence progression.
3. Station-8 colour/foot/hand coordination.
4. One balance/precision block from stations 9-11.
5. Slow clean chunk rehearsal.
6. Short linked-station rehearsal; do not add a second memory test at the end.

This session remains useful without a dedicated room.

## Session B - coordination and balance

Default load: low to moderate. Default duration: 35-55 minutes.

Choose 2-3 complementary blocks with full enough recovery to preserve precision:

- station 8 colour-foot-hand mapping and basketball rhythm;
- station 9 stable-to-unstable proprioception and object transfer;
- station 10 correct-foot skipping-rope patterns;
- station 11 racket-ball precision and controlled turns.

Include one active memory game. Do not add a high-intensity finisher; Monday CrossFit,
the prescribed room session, and the outdoor intervals already supply conditioning load.

## Technique-session integration variant

This replaces Session A for the week when longer chunks or an eligible mock are due. It
is not a sixth principal day. Default load: moderate. Default duration: 35-60 minutes.

Possible structures:

- opening chunk 1-3;
- heavy/interleaved chunk 4-7;
- coordination/balance chunk 8-9;
- precision finish 10-11;
- linked half circuit;
- fatigue primer followed by one precision station;
- exact full mock when the exact-room gate passes.

Record outcome and fidelity. For approximation layouts, show trend only and do not
compare the time with 6:15.

## Station 2 - obstacle/box confidence progression

Purpose: reduce hesitation while building repeatable, controlled crossings.

Levels are recorded independently; the user can move down a level without losing history.

1. **Sequence visualization:** approach, hand/foot placement concept, cross, place ball,
   return; mental fidelity.
2. **Approach and stop:** accelerate to a floor line, establish control, step away.
3. **Ground boundary:** cross a taped rectangle or very low marker while carrying a ball.
4. **Low stable step-over:** controlled crossing of an appropriate low, stable obstacle.
5. **Repeated low crossings:** alternate outward ball carry and empty return while keeping
   the official 5-out/4-back count.
6. **Progressive-height practice:** only with a stable purpose-built obstacle and enough
   space; retain clean landings and official repetition pattern.
7. **Verified obstacle:** use the official or confirmed equivalent obstacle and record
   exact fidelity.
8. **Linked opening chunk:** station 1 effort into station 2, then station 3 when available.

The UI records fear/confidence before and after, hesitation, clean repetitions, obstacle
level, and fidelity. It never tells the user to close their eyes physically.

## Station 8 - basketball coordination progression

Critical mapping is always visible during learning and hidden during recall testing:

- yellow = feet together, no dribble;
- blue = right foot, right-hand dribble;
- red = left foot, left-hand dribble.

Progression:

1. Verbal colour-to-action flash cards.
2. Floor-marker foot pattern without a ball.
3. Carry basketball through the marker pattern.
4. Stationary matching-side dribble.
5. Slow foot-and-dribble combination.
6. Direction change through a short pattern.
7. Random colour sequence.
8. Short fatigue primer followed by the sequence.
9. Verified official hoop layout and full outward/return task.

Record mapping errors, foot errors, dribble errors, prompts, and clean completion. Speed
progression occurs only after clean mapping.

## Stations 9-11 - balance and precision progression

Balance work must use varied, purposeful proprioception rather than only repeating a
line walk. Choose 2-3 drills that preserve quality: stable-to-unstable progression,
single-leg control, controlled reach/rotation, object transfer, and finally the
station-specific task. The balance board is a support exercise, not proof of station-9
success. Keep a stable support nearby and never add visual deprivation during physical
practice.

### Station 9: mobile bench

- tandem line walk;
- stable low beam/platform walk;
- mount, establish both feet, then take an object;
- controlled turn without supporting on the object or marker;
- object exchange and return;
- verified mobile-bench sequence when available.

Useful support drills include single-leg stance with clock reaches, tandem walk with
controlled head turns, slow step-and-hold, balance-board two-foot holds, small weight
shifts on the balance board, and object transfers while stable. Progress by reducing
hand support, increasing reach, or adding a light task—not by closing the eyes on an
unstable device.

Approximation drills must use an appropriate stable surface and must not be called mobile-
bench completion.

### Station 10: skipping rope

- basic rhythm;
- isolated displayed patterns;
- short blocks with a deliberate reset;
- random card selection;
- repeat only the failed current block, matching the official correction rule;
- perform after a brief movement primer.

Record pattern, errors, block restarts, and whether the card was known in advance.

### Station 11: racket and ping-pong ball

- static central balance on the racket;
- straight walk;
- turns;
- low markers/obstacles;
- recovery at the drop point;
- short fatigue primer followed by the course;
- verified official obstacle layout when available.

Record drops, incorrect grip, resume-point errors, time, and fidelity.

Useful support drills include carrying a light object while tandem walking, racket-ball
holds during slow weight shifts, controlled turns around a marker, and a short precision
course after a safe balance-board activation.

## Stations 1 and 3 - opening speed and climbing

Without a room:

- mark 18 m where space permits;
- accelerate, decelerate, and return through a simple slalom;
- practise pole order and entry direction;
- use appropriate existing climbing/step equipment only when it accurately maps to the
  wall-bar task;
- otherwise train the transition mentally and related pulling/step-up qualities as an
  approximation.

Do not invent wall-bar dimensions or a substitute that is presented as exact.

## Stations 4-7 - heavy work and dexterity

### Station 4

- suitable sandbag/mannequin drag when available;
- drag around a marker and return behind a line;
- train start grip and direction change;
- preserve the rule prohibiting the handle behind the head in recall quizzes.
- when the official load is unavailable, drag a confirmed lighter object over a short
  course and practise start grip, body angle, direction change, and finish position;
  label the result as an approximation.

### Stations 5-6

- push an appropriate sled/trolley when available;
- immediately perform colour/size sorting and placement;
- pull back;
- remove and return objects;
- quiz the `5 -> 6 -> 5 -> 6` alternation.

### Station 7

- seated or braced rope pull with suitable equipment;
- alternate sides only when the drill objective requires it;
- exact fidelity requires the verified 30 kg bag and course arrangement;
- train grip and transition into the coordination block.

## Session D - CrossFit-room explosive intervals

One self-directed session per week. Default duration: 35-50 minutes including warm-up;
the quality block is short. Purpose: produce repeated explosive efforts while heart rate
rises, then recover enough to keep movement fast and controlled.

Use one structure per session, not all of them:

1. **Ladder acceleration:** 2-3 ladder patterns, immediate 8-12 m acceleration, walk-back
   recovery; 2-3 sets of 3 repetitions.
2. **Box and shuttle:** 3-5 controlled box step-overs or low jumps, then a short shuttle;
   4-6 repetitions with 60-90 seconds recovery. Choose a box height that preserves quiet,
   stable landings and reduces station-2 hesitation.
3. **Police micro-circuit:** 10-15 seconds ladder, 10-15 seconds box/obstacle work, then
   10-20 seconds mapped push, pull, drag, or carry; 4-6 rounds with 90-150 seconds
   recovery.

The engine chooses one primary explosive pattern and one station-linked secondary
pattern. Mapped secondary work may include a short drag/carry, push/pull, rope pull, or
change-of-direction task for stations 1-7. It does not create a long generic workout.
Stop the quality block when two
consecutive repetitions show slower acceleration, unstable landing, or a technical
error. Record work duration, recovery duration, completed repetitions, perceived effort,
landing/hesitation quality, peak heart rate when available, and heart-rate drop after one
minute. Heart rate describes the response; it is not a reason to continue poor-quality
repetitions.

## Session E - outdoor explosive intervals

One running session per week. Default duration: 30-45 minutes including 10-15 minutes of
easy warm-up, drills, and an easy cool-down. Select one quality block:

- 8-10 x 15 seconds fast uphill, walk/jog back for 75-120 seconds;
- 6-8 x 20 seconds fast on flat ground, 80-100 seconds easy recovery;
- 2 sets of 4 x 20 seconds shuttle or change-of-direction work, 60 seconds between
  repetitions and 3 minutes between sets.

Begin below maximal sprint speed and preserve posture, foot placement, and controlled
deceleration. Progress by adding at most one or two quality repetitions, then by making
repetitions slightly longer; reduce recovery only after the whole session remains crisp.
Record repetition completion, perceived speed quality, effort, peak heart rate when
available, one-minute recovery, and any technical breakdown. This session is the week's
only run and replaces the former easy trail-maintenance session. The 11 October trail
replaces it during event week.

## Balance and coordination support blocks

Session B selects 2-3 related drills from station 8 and stations 9-11, with full enough
recovery for clean execution. It may be shortened to 20-35 minutes when fatigue from
CrossFit or an explosive session remains. It improves precision and circuit fluency and
does not contain another hard finisher.

## Monday CrossFit class

The app reserves Monday and does not invent the coach's workout. After the class, record
duration, emphasis, effort 1-5, notes, and overlap tags for grip, jumping, heavy legs, or
hard conditioning. These inputs may reduce or move the prescribed room or outdoor
interval session; they never create an extra training day.

## Embedded memory games

Exactly one short game appears inside each police session; no separate test is appended
at the end. Rotate among:

1. **Video detective:** watch a short official clip, pause, choose the next station or
   identify the critical rule.
2. **Circuit puzzle:** reorder shuffled station cards by drag-and-drop or tap-to-swap.
3. **Rule match:** pair stations with actions, prohibitions, repetitions, or correction
   rules.
4. **Transition choice:** choose what happens immediately before/after a station,
   including the `5 -> 6 -> 5 -> 6` alternation.
5. **Find the mistake:** correct one deliberately wrong step in a proposed sequence.

The weekly combination must progressively lead to unprompted mental reproduction of the
full circuit. Physical eyes-closed practice is prohibited.
