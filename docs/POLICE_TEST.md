# Police physical aptitude test - local authoritative reference

This document gives the application a stable, source-grounded description of the test.
It is a product reference, not a substitute for current instructions from the recruiter
on test day. The source register and local copies are in `docs/police-v1/` and
`docs/sources/police/`.

## Test-level rules

- The circuit contains 11 stations.
- Time and penalties/corrections affect the result.
- Each station must be performed according to its instructions.
- The recruiter identifies errors and requires correction.
- A station not attempted, not completed correctly, or abandoned causes failure (grade E).
- The published time limit in the official circuit sheet is 6 minutes 15 seconds.

the athlete's working targets:

- current reported complete-circuit time: 8:30;
- personal target: 6:11;
- official limit: 6:15;
- progress benchmark: clean completion first, time second.

## Official order and critical rules

### 1. Slalom between poles

1. Put on the protective vest.
2. Run to the end of the poles; the official video labels the run as 18 m.
3. Return through the slalom.

### 2. Obstacle crossing

1. Cross the obstacle carrying a tennis ball.
2. Place the ball in the red container on the other side.
3. Cross back without a ball.
4. Repeat.

The obstacle is crossed five times outward with a tennis ball and four times back without
a ball.

### 3. Wall bars

1. Cross the wall bars.
2. Go around the cones.
3. Cross the wall bars again.

The official sheet permits a normal forward/backward descent or a jump. The “bascule”
technique is prohibited.

### 4. Mannequin movement

1. Take hold of the mannequin, but not by the handle behind the head.
2. Drag it to the pole in the middle of the room.
3. Go around the pole.
4. Return the mannequin behind the white line.

### 5 and 6. Trolley and nuts - alternating sequence

These two station numbers form an interleaved sequence:

1. Push the trolley from the red cone to the far end of the room.
2. Choose one colour, take one large and two small nuts, and place them on the three
   matching bolts.
3. Pull the trolley from the far end back to the red-cone start.
4. Remove the nuts and return them to the container.

The app must teach and quiz the interleaving, not incorrectly present 5 and 6 as two
fully consecutive isolated tasks.

### 7. Rope pull

1. Pull the 30 kg bag using the rope.
2. Cross the room.
3. Pull the 30 kg bag again.

The candidate may brace the legs against the boxes.

### 8. Hoops and basketball

1. Carry the basketball and cross the hoops according to their colours.
2. On return, dribble on the side of the requested foot according to the colours.

Colour mapping:

- yellow: feet together, no dribble;
- blue: right foot, right-hand dribble;
- red: left foot, left-hand dribble.

### 9. Mobile bench

1. Mount the bench.
2. Take the ball after placing both feet.
3. Cross the bench.
4. Exchange the balls.
5. Return to the start.
6. Put down the ball.
7. Cross the bench once more without the ball.

If the candidate falls, resume where the fall occurred. The ball or cone must not be used
as support while turning.

### 10. Skipping rope

Perform the pattern shown on the randomly selected wall card. If an error occurs, repeat
the current block until it is completed correctly.

The local source image shows three example blocks, but V1 must not infer a complete set
of possible test-day cards beyond what the source establishes.

### 11. Racket and ping-pong ball obstacle course

1. Balance the ping-pong ball on the centre of the racket.
2. Cross the obstacles.

If the ball falls, pick it up and resume where it fell. Hold the racket by its handle with
a closed hand.
