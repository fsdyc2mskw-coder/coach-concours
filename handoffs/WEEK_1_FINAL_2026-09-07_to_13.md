## Friday 11 September — Police technique + AMRAP (version 3, the athlete's change request of 10 Sep)

Type: `police_technique` with a floating conditioning block. Location: home / outdoor
(ladder, rope, tennis balls, racket). Duration ≈ 40 min.

```
 1 warm-up ─► 2 memory ─► 3 station-2 ball crossing ─► 4 racket FRESH ref ─► 5 AMRAP 10 ─► 6 station-8 colours ─► 7 finish
   5 min       4 min          8 min                       5 min                 10 min         5 min                  2 min
```

the athlete's decisions behind version 3 (precedence rule 1):
- No box jumps on Friday: box was trained fresh on Thursday, one station-2 drill per day.
- Circuit memory: **never ask the athlete to explain the circuit.** Visualising and reciting the
  order is fine; the 3-station detail check (action, completion condition, next) is fine.
- Station-2 work uses the **official tennis-ball rule**, obstacle or no obstacle.
- The landing rehearsal is dropped. In its place: a 10-min AMRAP (HIIT, explosiveness,
  stamina), placed **after** the fresh racket reference so the reference stays fresh.
- Version 2's "no AMRAP on Friday" restriction is lifted. New rules R-WS-16 and R-WS-17
  (`rules/weekly_shape.md` v2, 10 Sep): every police session has one HIIT block; in a
  skill session it is ≤ 10 min and the last block before the cool-down. R-WS-09 amended:
  the day before the trail the block is the short version. Saturday stays easy, run-only.

### 1. Easy warm-up — 5 min
Walking, ankle circles, hip circles, shoulder rotations, controlled side-steps, 20 s of
mini-jumps over a line front/back and 20 s side to side (police warm-up, S2).

### 2. Circuit memory — 4 min
- Eyes closed, visualise the room and walk the circuit in your head, station 1 → 11.
- Recite the 11 stations in order, out loud.
- Pick 3 stations: say the action, what counts as done, and the next station.
- Record: forgotten, reversed, wrong transitions. Nobody asks for explanations.

### 3. Station 2, tennis-ball crossing — ≈ 8 min
Official rule (S1): cross the obstacle **with a tennis ball in hand**, put it in the red box
on the other side, cross back **without ball**; 5 crossings out, 4 back.

Set-up: a "start box" with 5 tennis balls, a "red box" (any container) 4-6 m away, and
the obstacle in between if one is ready (low bench, log, line on the ground). No obstacle:
same drill, same rule, walk or jog the distance.

- 2 rounds of the full 9-leg sequence, walking pace, hand-off clean into the box.
- 1 round at jogging pace, still clean.
- 60-90 s rest between rounds.
- Record: balls fumbled or dropped, hesitations at the obstacle.
- Approximation, not the official station: obstacle height and distance are not the official ones.

### 4. Fresh racket-ball precision reference — ≈ 5 min
3 × 1 min walking with the ball balanced on the racket centre, racket held by the handle,
closed hand (S1 wording). 30 s between rounds. Eyes open, walk continuously, controlled
turns; if the ball falls, pick it up and restart where it fell.
Record drops in round 1, 2, 3. This is the **standalone fresh reference** for Week 1;
Thursday recorded no fatigued value, so the comparison moves to Week 2.

### 5. AMRAP 10 min — explosiveness and stamina (floating conditioning block)
As many rounds as possible in 10 minutes of:
- 60 rope skips (single-unders, freestyle allowed)
- outdoor ladder up and down, twice
- 10 jump squats (police programme S2, séance 6: start in the squat, jump as high as possible, land back in the squat, cushion the landing with the whole body)

Record: rounds completed (+ partial), whether the ladder descent stayed controlled, and
whether the jump-squat landings stayed soft (this is the landing practice the athlete asked for).
Stop the round early if a ladder descent or a landing gets sloppy. No "bascule" on the ladder (S1,
station 3 rule).

### 6. Station 8 coordination approximation, now under fatigue — ≈ 5 min
Colour mapping until basketball and markers are bought: yellow = feet together;
blue = right foot + say "right hand"; red = left foot + say "left hand".
3 × 45 s, 30 s recovery. Coordination approximation only; doing it after the AMRAP turns
it into a coordination-under-fatigue exposure.

### 7. Finish — 2 min
Easy walk while replaying the circuit station 1 → 11 in your head.
