## Thu 1 Oct: SKILL SESSION, racket

```
 1 WARM-UP + 3 BROAD JUMPS                           8 min
   easy jog, ankles, wrists, shoulders; 3 jumps, landing held
 2 MEMORY, M1                                         6 min
   the 11 stations in order + the action at each, out loud
   last 2 min: recall check, 33 items   SCORE: errors
 3 SKILL BLOCK, racket                               18 min
   racket on the wobble board                  7 min
     build-up: hand on the wall → racket, no ball → ball on, hand off the wall
     then 3 × 30 s, 30 s rest
     SCORE: drops per 30 s · board touchdowns
   walk it off                                 1 min
   racket over obstacles                      10 min
     45 s flat · 10 s · 30 s obstacles · 10 s · 45 s flat
     2 sets, 90 s rest
     SCORE: drops flat 1 · obstacles · flat 2
     gaze: eyes on the ball, each obstacle looked at ONCE
 4 CARDIO, AMRAP 12                                  12 min
   slalom 18 m, one pass out and back
   wall bars, 3 passes, climb and jump
   skipping: 10 both feet · 5 right · 5 left (a catch = restart the block)
   no target. WRITE: rounds done, effort
 5 COOL-DOWN                                          5 min
```

## Fri 2 Oct: CHAIN SESSION, chain A

```
 1 WARM-UP + 3 BROAD JUMPS                           8 min
 2 FRESH REFERENCE, balance ladder                    3 min
   1 min  one leg, eyes closed, near a wall (30 s each leg)
   1 min  two feet on the wobble board, ball around the waist
   SCORE: touchdowns + ball drops
 3 MEMORY, M1                                         4 min
   the 11 stations: order + action
 4 CHAIN A, moderate                                 12 min
   3 rounds, 90 s rest between rounds
   slalom 18 m → walk → wall bars, 3 passes → walk
   → 5 hoops, ball in the arms, one pass
   → TAIL A: one leg, ball around the waist, 30 s (switch leg each round)
   the walks ARE the training: shoulders down, breathe, arrive ready
   SCORE: time per round · cones touched · hoop foot errors · tail A touchdowns
 5 CARDIO, EMOM 12                                   12 min
   min 1  carioca, 18 m out and back
   min 2  skipping: 10 both · 5 right · 5 left
   min 3  8 half burpees
   × 4. no target. WRITE: held 12 min yes/no, effort
 6 TAIL B, balance ladder                             5 min
   4 × 1 min, 20 s between
   min 1  one leg, eyes open
   min 2  one leg, eyes closed, near a wall
   min 3  one leg, ball around the waist
   min 4  two feet on the wobble board, ball around the waist
   SCORE: touchdowns + drops per minute, against the reference
   STOP if dizzy or 3 touchdowns in one minute; a shortened tail still counts
 7 COOL-DOWN                                          5 min
```
