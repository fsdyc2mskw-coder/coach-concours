## Purpose
Train tolerance to the breathless state that follows a burst (her reported weakness, 11 Oct), after
the Woorons method (repeated sprints at low lung volume).

## Execution
Breathe out fully · sprint 30 m without breathing in · breathe when needed · walk back 30 s.
Hold: W6 10-15 m · W7 15-20 m · W8 the full 30 m if clean · then 2 sets.

## Stop / regress if
- dizziness → stop the set. Never take fast deep breaths before a rep. On land only, away from
  traffic.

## Sources
