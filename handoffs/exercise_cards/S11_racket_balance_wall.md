## Execution
Ball on the racket centre, closed fist on the handle. Weave around 5-6 low obstacles (shoes, cones),
then walk along a FLAT low wall (never a round or wet top). Gaze: eyes on the ball, each obstacle
looked at once, never at the feet. A drop: pick it up and resume where it fell.
Stop: wobbling on the wall → step down on purpose.

Fits: the skill block, fresh. Trains: station 11 with the balance demand of station 9.
First pass (W7 Tuesday): 3 passes on the wall, drops counted.
**This drill is an approximation of the official station.**
