HIIT filler. Flagged HIIT: the purpose is heart rate and stamina, not skill.

## Purpose (one sentence)

Drive the heart rate hard and keep leg power available under fatigue, which is what the slalom and the change of direction cost late in the circuit.

## Set-up

Nothing. Flat, even ground with grip. Not on a slippery or uneven surface.

## Execution

1. Feet shoulder width.
2. Squat to about parallel.
3. Drive up and leave the ground.
4. Land soft, absorb into the next squat, no pause at the bottom.

Dose: 20 seconds of work, or 10 reps.

## Quality criteria (what "clean" means)

- Landing is soft and quiet, knees bending on contact.
- Knees stay out, no inward collapse on landing.
- Depth stays the same from the first rep to the last.

## Stop / regress if

- landings turn loud or stiff;
- the knees fall inward on any rep;
- depth shortens to a hop.

Regress to air squats for the rest of the block rather than finishing badly.

## Progress to next card when

- 10 clean reps inside 20 seconds for four consecutive rounds, landings still quiet.

## Dose anchor

No recorded number yet. First exposure is a measure, not a workout: one clean 20-second count, written into the ledger. The athlete may override and go straight to the dose, as she did for station 3.

## Block format

Works as an EMOM minute, never as the last atom of a block when the legs are already spent. Trains conditioning and leg power. **This drill is an approximation, not an official station.**
