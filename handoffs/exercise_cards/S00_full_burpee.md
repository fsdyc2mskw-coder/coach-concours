## Purpose
Level-up of the half burpee (her EMOMs were too easy): ground to standing to jump, the burst that
makes her breathless, trained on purpose.

## Execution
Squat, hands down · feet back · chest to the floor · feet back in · jump. Breathe out on the push.

## Quality criteria
- Chest touches, full hip extension at the top.

## Stop / regress if
- lower back sags → half burpee.

## Official reference
General conditioning, no single station. **This drill is an approximation, not the official station.**
