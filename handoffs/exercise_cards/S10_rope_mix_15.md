## Purpose
Replaces the skipping EMOM 8 she found dull (25 Oct). Trains her hard part, one-foot skipping,
and the official random card. Two parts only (her decision).

## Execution (text for the app)
Part A · Tabata, 8 min. 20 s skip, 10 s rest, 16 rounds (the 8-round sequence twice):
- rounds 1-2 basic, both feet together, small hops
- rounds 3-4 side to side, feet together, 20 cm hops left and right
- rounds 5-6 front to back, feet together, 20 cm hops
- round 7 left foot only · round 8 right foot only
- rounds 9-16 the same again
Rest 1 min.
Part B · card ladder, 6 min. 1 min to do each card, 30 s rest. An error restarts the block you are in.
1. 5 right · 5 left · 10 together (the official demo card)
2. 8 right · 8 left · 10 together
3. 10 right · 10 left · 10 together
4. 15 right · 15 left · 15 together

## Score
Part A: misses on the one-foot rounds (7, 8, 15, 16). Part B: cards finished clean, out of 4.

## Stop if
Calf or Achilles pain.

## Progress when
4 / 4 clean cards on two sessions → card 4 becomes 20 · 20 · 15.
