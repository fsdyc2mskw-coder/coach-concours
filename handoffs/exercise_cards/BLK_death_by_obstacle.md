## Format
Minute n = n obstacle crossings + n full burpees + n bar rows; the rest of the minute is rest. The
first minute not finished is the "death": from then on repeat the last completed level until 7:00.
Score: highest minute completed. Followed within 30 s by the tired test.
Rule: a sloppy crossing → 2 box jumps on the low block instead, for the rest of the block.
