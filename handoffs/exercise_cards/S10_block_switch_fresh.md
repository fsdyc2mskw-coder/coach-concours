```

 5x PIED GAUCHE     5 skips on the LEFT foot
 10x PIEDS JOINTS   10 skips with the FEET TOGETHER
```

This is now the default order of the card. Version 2 used "10 both, 5 right, 5 left" from
memory; that order is retired as the default. The official text still says the card is drawn
at random, so another card on the day may change the order: the coin variant below keeps that
open. Version 2 (23 Sep): skipping under fatigue kept as wanted. The id keeps its old name so
nothing that points at it breaks.

## Purpose (one sentence)

Train the exact moment the station tests: starting straight on one foot, switching right to left
and then to feet together without stopping the rope, fresh to learn it, tired because that is how
it comes in the test.

## Set-up

- Skipping rope, ends reach the armpits when standing on the middle.
- Flat floor, 2 m free around you.
- The card written on paper and taped at eye height, like the wall in the test:
  `5 DROIT · 5 GAUCHE · 10 JOINTS`. Read it before every round.
- A coin for the variant rounds only.
- Timer: 45 s work, 30 to 45 s rest.

## Execution (skill block, fresh)

1. Read the card out loud.
2. Rope on: 5 right foot, 5 left foot, 10 feet together. That is one card.
3. Repeat the card until the 45 s end.
4. Switch on the next turn of the rope, no extra hop, no pause.
5. Count out loud, restarting the count at each block (1 to 5, 1 to 5, 1 to 10).
6. An error (rope catch, wrong foot, wrong count): stand still 2 s, breathe, restart the
   block you were in, not the whole card, as in the test.
7. 4 to 6 rounds. The last round of the skill block is a VARIANT: flip the coin, heads =
   the official order, tails = 5 left · 5 right · 10 together.

## In a chain or cardio block (tired)

- One piece = ONE card: 5 right, 5 left, 10 together (20 skips, about 30 s at her 40 skips
  per minute).
- An error = restart the block you were in. It costs time, never a penalty.
- Score in a chain block: restarts per card. In a cardio block: nothing beyond rounds and effort.
- Never in the cardio block of the week when skipping is the skill focus (R-TL-10).

## Quality criteria (what "clean" means)

- the first skip is already on the right foot, no warm-up skip on two feet;
- the rope never stops between blocks;
- the count is right (5, 5, 10), no extra skip;
- bounce 2 to 5 cm, wrists turn the rope, shoulders quiet;
- the free foot stays close to the standing foot on the one-foot blocks.

## Stop / regress if

- two restarts at the same switch in one round: next round, 5 right then 10 together only,
  then 5 left then 10 together, until clean;
- knees or calves hurt: stop the card for the day;
- the count is lost: slow the rope, keep the order.

## Progress to next card when

- 6 rounds with 0 or 1 restart in total, on two separate sessions, at confidence 4 or more.

## Recorded numbers this card starts from

- both feet: 40 skips per minute, EMOM real capacity (11 Sep);
- one foot: about 15 clean skips maximum, fresh (17 Sep). The official block is 5, so the
  dose sits at about 33 % of capacity. Starting on one foot (version 3) is the new difficulty.

## Official reference

Station 10, rule text from `01_Official_police_sources/01_PARCOURS_11_stations_official_(Explications-sport).pdf`,
panel "Corde à sauter": the candidate skips as shown on the card on the wall, the card is
chosen at random, and an error means restarting the current block until it succeeds.
Card order from the official demo video (YouTube, Police cantonale vaudoise), frame sent by
the athlete on 1 October 2026: 5 right foot, 5 left foot, 10 feet together.
**This drill is an approximation, not the official station.**

## Block format it fits

Skill block (fresh), chain block (tired), and cardio block (tired) except in a skipping week.
Skills trained: coordination, one-foot balance, rhythm, counting under fatigue.
