## Format
Each lap: slalom about 15 m (out straight, weave back through the poles) · obstacle over and back
(2 crossings) · burpees that climb: lap 1 = 2, lap 2 = 4, lap 3 = 6, then 8, 10 ... until 7:00.
Score: last lap reached + burpees done in it. Pace: lap 1 at about 80 %, then build.
Followed within 30 s by the tired test (hoops + balance on bench with ball).
Rule: a sloppy crossing → 2 box jumps on the low block instead, for the rest of the block.
Rematch in week 10. **This block is an approximation, not an official station.**
