## Purpose
Upper-body pulling that stands in for the wall bars (3) and the rope pull (7); her strength
stand-in alongside push-ups (decision 12 Oct).

## Execution
Under a bar at hip to chest height, heels on the ground, body like a plank · pull the chest to the
bar · lower slowly. Harder: feet further forward.

## Stop / regress if
- hips sag → stand more upright.

## Official reference
Stations 3 and 7, strength only. **This drill is an approximation, not the official station.**
