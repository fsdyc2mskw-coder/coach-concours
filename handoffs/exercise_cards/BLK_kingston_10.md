## Why
The police test is about 6 min non-stop over 11 different stations, with precision needed while out
of breath. One lap = 6 stations in the same order as the real test, done fast, again and again.
Built for Kingston (no wall, no bar, no gym), W8 (25 Oct).

## Set-up (5 min)
A 20 m lane (two shoes). Next to it: the yoga mat flat for burpees and push-ups, the 11 hoops in the
official colour order, the yoga mat folded for balance, the rope. Everything a few steps apart.

## One lap, in this order
1. Shuttle sprint 4 × 20 m (out, back, out, back). Replaces sprint and slalom: the turns are the slalom.
2. Five times: 1 burpee (chest to the floor) + 1 squat jump.
3. Ten push-ups, chest to the floor.
4. Hoops, 11, out, footwork by colour (yellow both feet, blue right, red left). An error: back to hoop 1.
5. One leg on the folded mat, tennis ball around the waist: 10 s right + 10 s left. A touchdown restarts that leg.
6. Skipping card: 5 right, 5 left, 10 together. An error restarts the block.
Then straight back to 1. Expect about 2 min per lap (4-5 laps).

## Score
Laps + station reached at 10:00. Effort 1-5.

## Rules
No planned rest. Dizzy → stop, it still counts. Normally followed within 30 s by the fatigue test
(R-SP-15); W8 without it (her decision, 25 Oct). No hoops → station 4 = 10 one-leg skips (5 per leg).

Fits: the enchaînement. Trains: breath and speed of the test, transitions, precision under fatigue.
**This block is an approximation, not an official station.**
