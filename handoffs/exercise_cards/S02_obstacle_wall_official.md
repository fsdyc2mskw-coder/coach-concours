## Purpose
Cross the wall at about the exam height, cleanly and without hesitation, in the official rhythm, so
that station 2 (her biggest fear) becomes routine.

## Set-up
The park wall she crossed on 11 Oct (roughly under her chest, about the exam height, her words).
Soft granulate landing. A cap or bag on the far side as the "red box". 5 tennis balls at the start.

## Execution
1. 4 crossings from a walk.
2. 4 crossings from a 5 m run-up.
3. Official round: take a ball, cross, drop it in the red box, cross back without; 5 out, 4 back.
4. 1 min rest, repeat the round (3 to 5 rounds).
Cues: eyes on the top of the wall · both hands firm · land on two feet, knees soft · breathe out
over the top.

## Quality criteria
- No pause in front of the wall. Two-foot landing, knees bent. Ball never thrown.

## Stop / regress if
- two hesitations in a row → back one step; crossings get sloppy → end the rounds (a shortened
  block counts); wet wall → no session on the wall.

## Progress when
- 5 official rounds with 0 hesitations → the wall goes inside the storm blocks (fatigued).

## Official reference
Station 2: "Franchir l'obstacle avec une balle dans la main, la poser dans la boîte rouge de l'autre
côté, franchir l'obstacle au retour, sans balle. L'obstacle est franchi 5x à l'aller (avec une balle
de tennis) et 4x au retour (sans balle de tennis)."
**This drill is an approximation, not the official station.**
