## Purpose
Upper-body pushing; with the bar row, the strength stand-in for the wall bars and the trolley
(decision 12 Oct). Used in WILL 10 and the travel storms.

## Execution
Body straight, hands under the shoulders · chest to the floor · hands lift off briefly · push back
up breathing out · abs tight.

## Stop / regress if
- hips sag → hands on the low wall.

**This drill is an approximation, not the official station.**
