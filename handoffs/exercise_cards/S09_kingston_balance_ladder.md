## Purpose
Balance with the ball, trained fresh and tired when no bench, wall or wobble board is around.
Written for Kingston (W8, 25 Oct) after the athlete killed walking a flat gym bench (too easy).
The folded mat replaces the wobble board of the balance ladder.

## Set-up
Yoga mat folded in four · 1 tennis ball · a wall · a timer (1 min work, 30 s rest).

## Execution (text for the app)
Stand tall, knee soft, eyes on a fixed point. The free foot does not touch the standing leg.
1. Right leg on the mat. Pass the tennis ball around the waist, hand to hand, without stopping. 1 min.
2. Left leg on the mat. Same. 1 min.
3. Eyes closed, on the floor (not on the mat), one hand near the wall. 30 s right, then 30 s left.
4. Right leg on the mat, 1 m from the wall. Throw the ball underarm at the wall, catch it with one hand. 1 min.
5. Left leg on the mat. Same. 1 min.
6. Repeat 1 and 2. 2 min.

Tired version (5 min, after cardio): steps 1 and 2 only, 30 s each leg, 3 rounds, 20 s rest.

## Score
Touchdowns (free foot touches the ground) + ball drops, per minute. Aim 0.

## Stop / regress if
Dizzy · 3 touchdowns in one minute (it still counts) · any ankle pain: stop for the day.

## Progress when
0 touchdowns on steps 4-5 on two sessions → eyes closed on the floor step becomes eyes closed on the mat
(near the wall).

Fits: skill block (fresh), tired balance after cardio. Trains: station 9 balance, ball handling.
Research: balance is task-specific (Kümmel et al., Sports Medicine 2016).
