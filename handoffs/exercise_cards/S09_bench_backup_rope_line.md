## Execution
Rope laid straight. Heel to toe on it, eyes 2-3 m ahead. Ball A at the start, ball B at the far end:
take A, walk, swap A for B, turn on the line, walk back, put B down, one last walk without ball.
Used when no flat kerb or low wall exists. The log says "B".
