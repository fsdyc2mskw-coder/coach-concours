## Purpose
Walk, turn and handle a ball on a narrow raised surface, the exact task of station 9; balance is
task-specific (Kümmel et al., Sports Medicine 2016), so one-leg standing alone does not transfer.
Name chosen by the athlete (12 Oct).

## Set-up
A low brick wall with a flat top, dry. Ball A at the start, ball B at the far end.

## Execution
Skill day: 3 walks, eyes 2-3 m ahead · 2 walks holding a ball · 1 official-style walk.
Official-style walk: both feet on → take ball A → walk → swap A for B → walk back → put B down →
one last walk without ball. Never lean on a ball to turn.
Test day (fresh and tired): 1 official-style walk.

## Quality criteria
- Eyes ahead, steady pace, turn without support, no touchdown.

## Stop / regress if
- wobbling → step down on purpose; dizzy after a storm → stop, breathe, then finish; wet cap → a
  line on the ground instead.

## Progress when
- 0 touchdowns fresh on two sessions → tired version counts; then hold the ball at chest height.

## Official reference
Station 9: "Monter sur le banc, prendre la balle après avoir posé les deux pieds, traverser le banc,
échanger les balles, revenir au départ, poser la balle, traverser une dernière fois le banc, sans
balle. Si le candidat tombe, il reprend d'où il est tombé. Il est interdit de s'appuyer sur la
balle, ou le cône, pour se tourner."
**This drill is an approximation, not the official station.** The wall does not move; the bench does.
