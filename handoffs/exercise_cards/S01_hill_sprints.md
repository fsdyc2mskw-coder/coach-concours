## Purpose (one sentence)

Short, all-out uphill efforts that build explosiveness and the first metres, safely,
because the slope shortens the stride and softens the impact.

## Set-up

- A short, steady slope, about 20 to 40 m of climb that can be run in 10 seconds.
- Placed at the END of the easy weekend run, legs warm.

## Execution

1. Finish the easy run at the foot of the slope.
2. Sprint uphill 8 to 10 seconds, driving the knees and the arms, body leaning in.
3. Walk back down slowly. Start the next one only when the breathing is back to easy.
4. 6, 8 or 10 reps, as the week says.
5. Jog 5 minutes easy to finish.

## Quality criteria (what "clean" means)

- every rep is fast from the first step;
- the last rep looks like the first; if it does not, the set is over.

## Stop / regress if

- the speed drops clearly: stop, the count is not a target;
- any pull in the calf or hamstring: stop at once.
