## Purpose (one sentence)

Wakes the legs up for fast, springy work at the start of every police session, and builds
the explosiveness her endurance background lacks.

## Set-up

- Grass or soft, flat ground, the same place each time if possible.
- A line on the ground to start from.

## Execution

1. Feet hip-width, toes behind the line.
2. Swing the arms back, then jump forward with both feet, as far as possible.
3. Land on both feet and HOLD the landing for a second.
4. Walk back to the line. That walk is the rest.
5. 3 or 5 jumps, as the week says.

## Quality criteria (what "clean" means)

- both feet leave and land together;
- the landing is held, knees soft, no step forward or back;
- every jump is a real effort, not a hop.

## Stop / regress if

- the landing cannot be held twice in a row: stop the slot for the day;
- knees, ankles or calves hurt: stop;
- Monday's class was tagged heavy_legs or jumping: skip the slot (R-AD-04).
