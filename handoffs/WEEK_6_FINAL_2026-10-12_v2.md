## Fri 16, ENCHAÎNEMENT at the park (to the limit)
Set-up first, all within about 20 m: slalom lane (about 15 m of poles, start marked) · your wall ·
bench (low wall, ball A at the start, ball B at the far end) · hoops line on the flat · burpee
spot · a bar hip to chest high for rows.
```
 3    SET-UP
 8    WARM-UP + PRIMING: 3 min jog through the poles · 2 min leg swings, lunges, arm circles,
      5 inchworms · 2 × 10 s flat sprint (priming) · 3 broad jumps
 3    FRESH REFERENCE (normal speed)
      8 HOOPS 5 hoops out (ball in arms) + back dribble, 1 pass           errors
      9 BALANCE ON BENCH WITH BALL, 1 official-style walk with the swap   touchdowns
 4    MEMORY card RÈGLES
 7    STORM 1 · "ACHERON PARK" (ladder, AMRAP 7)
      each lap: slalom (out straight, weave back) · obstacle over and back · burpees that climb:
      lap 1 = 2, lap 2 = 4, lap 3 = 6, lap 4 = 8, lap 5 = 10 ...
      score: last lap reached + burpees done in it
 3    TIRED TEST 1, within 30 s: 8 HOOPS (errors) · 9 BENCH WITH BALL (touchdowns)
 3    REST: walk, long exhales
 7    STORM 2 · "DEATH BY OBSTACLE" (every minute, 7 min)
      minute n = n obstacle crossings + n burpees + n rows under a bar; the rest of the minute
      is rest. The first minute not finished = the "death": from then on repeat the last
      completed level until 7:00
      score: highest minute completed
 3    TIRED TEST 2, within 30 s: 8 HOOPS · 9 BENCH WITH BALL
 5    COOL-DOWN: walk, calves, hips, 5 slow breaths with a long exhale
```
Rules for both storms: burpees chest to the floor, breathe out on the push · rows body straight,
chest to the bar · a sloppy crossing → box jumps on the low block for the rest of that storm ·
wobbly on the bench → step down on purpose · dizzy → stop, walk, it still counts.
Log: both storm scores · fresh, tired 1, tired 2 for hoops and bench (the GAPS are computed) ·
effort · box jumps used or not.
Rematch: both storms return in week 10; aim to beat these scores.
