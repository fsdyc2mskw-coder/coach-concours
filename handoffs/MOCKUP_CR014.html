Mockup CR-014 · déplacer les jours

Tap Modifier l'ordre, then drag a card by its handle onto another day. Works with a mouse and with a finger.

What to try

  • Drag Explosivité en salle (Friday) onto Saturday: the day holds two sessions, and only the stack flag speaks.
  • Move it on to Sunday: the stack flag clears and the other checks come back.
  • Drag Explosivité en salle onto Thursday, then the trail onto Friday: three training days in a row, two of them hard.
  • Try to drop something on Monday, Tuesday or Wednesday: refused, the date has passed.

The checks behind it

  • STACK two sessions on one day. While it lasts, nothing else is checked.
  • C1 two hard days side by side. The CrossFit and run-intervals pair is the accepted one, since you chose it on 15 September.
  • C2 three hard days in a row. Hard rule R-WS-10, so it is shown in red.
  • C3 more than three training days with no rest.
  • C4 a police session with a running HIIT the day after the run intervals (R-WS-08).
  • C5 the two runs side by side.
  • C6 the trail leaves le week-end (R-WS-03).

Monday says nothing when it moves. You know your class schedule.

Hard is read from the intensity bars the app already draws: three bars is hard, two is moderate, one is light.
